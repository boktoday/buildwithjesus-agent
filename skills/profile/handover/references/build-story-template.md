# The story of <build name>

One page. The student's words, polished - never replaced.

**The need** - two lines: who needed what, in their words.
**What I built** - two or three lines, plain language. The stuck
parts are allowed here; stories with a struggle are true ones.
**The give** - who received it, when, and what they said or did (with
their permission for names and quotes).
**What I learned** - two or three lines. Honest beats impressive.
**A photo** - optional. Ask before photographing anyone.

Built by <name>, <month year>. Made for free, and given away.

---

Rules, before it goes anywhere:

- **Their words stay.** Polish spelling and rhythm; keep the voice.
- **Truth only.** No embellishment. The story is evidence, not
  marketing.
- **Permission first.** For names, quotes, and photographs - ask the
  person. For anything public, a leader reviews first (the
  `handover` guardrails).
- **One page, maximum.** If it needs two, the build needs a
  narrower story.

Where it goes:

1. `builds-archive.md` - appended under the build's entry.
2. Optionally, a shareable copy for the church noticeboard, the
   family, or (with full sign-off) the site's examples.
3. It is also the proof the student shows when doing outreach -
   see the `outreach` skill.
