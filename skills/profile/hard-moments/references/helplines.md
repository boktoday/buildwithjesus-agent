# Helplines

The agent reads this file when a hard moment appears (see the
`hard-moments` skill) and shows the two or three most relevant lines -
plainly, tap-ready, not buried in a paragraph.

A trusted adult comes first: a parent, a leader, a school counsellor,
a doctor. These lines are the backup that is always awake.

## Australia (default)

- **Emergency - 000** (police, ambulance, fire)
- **Lifeline - 13 11 14** (24/7, any crisis, any age)
- **Kids Helpline - 1800 55 1800** (ages 5 to 25, 24/7)
- **Beyond Blue - 1300 22 4636** (mental health)
- **headspace - headspace.org.au** (ages 12 to 25)
- **13YARN - 13 92 76** (First Nations)
- **QLife - 1800 184 527** (LGBTQ+)

## Anywhere else in the world

The local emergency number, plus the verified directory at
**findahelpline.com** for the country's crisis lines.

## For families - localising this list

Replace the Australia list with your country's lines. Two ways:

1. Edit this file directly, or
2. Drop a `helplines.md` in the profile root - the agent checks
   there first and this file becomes the fallback.

Keep it short. The agent shows two or three lines, never the whole
page.
